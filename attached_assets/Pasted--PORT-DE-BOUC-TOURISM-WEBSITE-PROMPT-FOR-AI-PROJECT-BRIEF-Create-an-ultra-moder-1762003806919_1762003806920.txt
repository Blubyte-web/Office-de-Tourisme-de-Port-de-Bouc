# 🏖️ PORT-DE-BOUC TOURISM WEBSITE - PROMPT FOR AI

## 📋 PROJECT BRIEF

Create an **ultra-modern, premium tourism website** for Port-de-Bouc, France. The site must be visually stunning, with smooth 3D animations, fluid interactions, and a "WOW factor" that stops visitors in their tracks.

---

## 🎨 DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

### **Visual Style:**
- **Modern & Premium**: Think Apple, Awwwards-winning sites, high-end e-commerce
- **3D Elements**: Subtle 3D graphics, parallax effects, depth layers
- **Animations**: Smooth scroll-triggered animations, micro-interactions, hover effects
- **Color Palette**: Mediterranean vibes - deep blues (#0A4D8C), turquoise (#00BCD4), sandy beige (#F5E6D3), white (#FFFFFF), sunset orange (#FF6B35)
- **Typography**: Modern serif for headlines (Playfair Display, Lora), clean sans-serif for body (Inter, DM Sans)
- **Glassmorphism**: Frosted glass effects on cards and overlays
- **Dark Mode**: Optional toggle with smooth transition

### **Performance:**
- Butter-smooth 60fps animations
- Lazy loading images
- Optimized for mobile (responsive design)
- Fast page load (<2 seconds)
- No janky scrolling or stuttering

## 🏗️ SITE STRUCTURE

### **1. HERO SECTION** 🎬
**Design inspiration:** Full-screen immersive experience

**Features:**
- **3D Animated Background**: Subtle wave animation, parallax effect with multiple layers
- **Video Option**: Loop of Port-de-Bouc coastline (if available) OR animated gradient + 3D sphere/globe
- **Glassmorphic Hero Card**: Centered, with frosted glass effect
  - Main headline: "Port-de-Bouc: Votre Joyau Méditerranéen"
  - Subheading: "Découvrez la beauté de la côte provençale"
  - 2 CTAs: "Explorer" (primary), "Découvrir Plus" (secondary outline)
- **Scroll Indicator**: Animated bounce arrow at bottom
- **Parallax Effect**: Background moves slower than foreground on scroll

**Tech:** Framer Motion, Three.js (optional for 3D), CSS transforms

## 🎭 ANIMATIONS & INTERACTIONS

### **Must-have animations:**
1. **Smooth Scroll**: Scroll-triggered animations (fade-in, slide-up, scale)
2. **Parallax Backgrounds**: Hero section, section dividers
3. **3D Card Tilts**: On hover (use CSS transforms or react-tilt)
4. **Number Counters**: Animate statistics on scroll into view
5. **Image Zoom**: Hover effect on photos
6. **Loading Animations**: Skeleton loaders while content loads
7. **Page Transitions**: Smooth fade between sections
8. **Cursor Trail** (optional): Custom cursor with trail effect
9. **Scroll Progress Bar**: Top of page showing read progress

### **Libraries to use:**
- **Framer Motion** (React animations)
- **AOS** (Animate On Scroll)
- **GSAP** (advanced animations)
- **Three.js** (optional 3D graphics)
- **Swiper** (carousels)
- **Lightbox** (image galleries)
